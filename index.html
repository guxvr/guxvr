Article 2 · Anomaly detection

Unsupervised sensor-fault detection

A controlled comparison of a statistical threshold, PCA, and an LSTM autoencoder over 23,073 multivariate OBD-II telemetry windows and five injected fault classes.

Finding: the LSTM reached 0.5634 AUC-PR with mean aggregation, but the ranking nearly disappeared with max aggregation — evaluation design changed the verdict.

My role: training and calibration pipeline, metrics, PCA and LSTM autoencoder implementations, result analysis, and paper writing.

03
Article 3 · Retrieval-augmented generation

Chunking strategies for RAG in Portuguese

An experiment with multilingual E5, FAISS, and Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct comparing slide boundaries with 128, 256, and 512-token windows across seven configurations.

Finding: slides led MRR@5 at 0.87, while 512/0 led Recall@5 at 0.94 with higher context cost; 20% overlap showed no consistent advantage.

My role: deterministic token chunking, embedding and FAISS protocol, auditable retrieval evaluation, dataset validation, and final scientific revision.

  • Qwen2.5
